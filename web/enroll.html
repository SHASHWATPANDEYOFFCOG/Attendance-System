1 · Consent

Enrolment creates a mathematical template of your face. It is encrypted at rest and used only to mark attendance. A face template cannot be reissued if it leaks, unlike a password, so consent is explicit and revocable. Withdrawing consent deletes the template; your attendance history remains as an academic record.
